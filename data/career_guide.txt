GENERAL CAREER ADVICE FOR INDIAN STUDENTS
----------------------------
Resume Tips General:
- Keep resume to 1 page for freshers
- Include GitHub and LinkedIn profile links
- Quantify achievements such as improved performance by 30 percent
- List projects with tech stack and impact
- Include CGPA only if above 7.5
- Use action verbs like Developed Built Designed Led Implemented
- Use reverse chronological order for experience
- Include a professional summary of 2 to 3 lines at the top

Interview Tips General:
- Practice DSA on LeetCode and HackerRank for tech interviews
- Research the company before HR round
- Prepare STAR format answers Situation Task Action Result
- Dress professionally for in-person interviews
- Follow up with a thank you email post-interview
- Prepare 5 questions to ask the interviewer
- Know your resume content inside out

Salary Negotiation Tips for India:
- Research market salary on Glassdoor AmbitionBox LinkedIn Salary
- Do not reveal current salary if possible
- Ask for 20 to 30 percent hike during job change
- Consider Total Compensation CTC not just base salary
- Negotiate joining bonus if base salary is non-negotiable
- Get offer letter before resigning from current job

Higher Education Options in India:
- GATE score valid for PSU jobs and IIT NIT M.Tech admissions
- CAT for MBA at IIMs and top B-Schools
- UPSC for Civil Services IAS IPS IFS
- GRE or GMAT for MS or MBA abroad
- CLAT for Law school NLUs
- NEET for Medical MBBS MD
